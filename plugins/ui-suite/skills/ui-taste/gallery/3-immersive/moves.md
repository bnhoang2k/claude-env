# Rung 3 motion moves

Generated by a spike script from a motion-reference catalog (the rung 3 motion catalog, both passes). Do not edit by hand; regenerate it.
Copy the feel, never the layout.

228 moves whose rung fit names rung 3.

## Building blocks

### Things that notice you

- **A focal object turns toward the pointer with a damped lag, each part on its own range, and returns to rest when the pointer leaves** `a-focal-object-turns-toward-the-pointer-with-a-damped-lag`
  Rung fit: Rung 2 as a bare hover answer: lost-little-explorer shows no proven idle motion. Rung 3 when it keeps a low idle floor (agehana) or gets idle of its own (a blink or a breath), inside a scene that fills the frame, with a way in and out. Ask the user first if it is a 3D model.
  Travels to: Empty states, 404 and offline pages, sign-in forms, settings headers (a live preview that leans toward the hovered control), dashboards (a gauge or globe that leans toward the hovered row), maps (a compass that faces the cursor), shop products that turn toward the pointer. The graded-range idea alone travels to product viewers, map pins and tilt cards in tools.
  Reduce motion: The front-facing pose at rest (lost-little-explorer 0.24 s or 5.56 s, eyes on the viewer; agehana 3.65 s). Pointer tracking off.

- **A small part watches the visitor from inside a fixed shape** `a-small-part-watches-the-visitor-from-inside-a-fixed-shape`
  Rung fit: Rung 2 by itself. At rung 3 it is one reactive part inside a scene that also moves on its own.
  Travels to: A form helper that looks at the active field. Empty states. Assistant panels. A map marker that faces the pointer. A status mark on a dashboard.
  Reduce motion: Pupils centered and slightly down, looking at the main action.

- **A lone character roams the page on its own and drifts toward the pointer, a little late** `a-lone-character-roams-the-page-on-its-own-and-drifts-toward`
  Rung fit: Rung 3: it moves on its own and uses the whole frame. Not rung 4: the pointer attracts it but does not steer it. It lacks the way in and out. Ask first about the 3D model.
  Travels to: Empty states, 404 pages, onboarding welcome screens, about pages, loading waits.
  Reduce motion: The f01 pose, curled and still, placed beside the headline, not over it. Sparkles at fixed brightness.

- **The pointer uncovers a second layer of the subject, and the layer heals shut behind it** `pointer-uncovers-layer-that-heals`
  Rung fit: Weak as rung 3. Rung 2 answers end in under 0.4 s and leave no mark. Most clips here heal in 0.3 to 1 s, a short comet tail close to a rung 2 hover answer. Keep the move only if the spec sets a heal of 1 s or more and names Grid Runner as the model. Otherwise demote it to rung 2. Closest existing move: 'A surface changes only its color as the viewer tilts'. Vision Reveal shows no second layer and heals in about 0.3 to 0.5 s; it stays only as a contrast.
  Travels to: Docs (pipes under a wall, code under a button), Data chart (raw points under a smooth line), Map (old map under new), Shop material, photo before and after.
  Reduce motion: A toggle swaps layers in one cut, or a fixed split shows both.

- **A lens under the pointer swells one spot of a dense field, and a fixed readout names what sits at its center** `fisheye-lens-names-the-center`
  Rung fit: Rung 3, not rung 2. Rung 2 picks one item and shows it in a separate panel; the field never changes shape. Here the whole field warps as one surface, and the lens has a growth tail of about 2.5 s, which clearly clears rung 2's 0.07 to 0.38 s. A lens that only scales one item would be a rung 2 hover. It sharpens differences 2 and 3. The fixed top readout is seen only on mobile; none was seen on desktop.
  Travels to: Data chart (a dense scatter where the lens opens the crowded spot), a Docs index of every page, a Shop catalog.
  Reduce motion: No lens. Hover outlines the nearest item and the readout changes at once.

- **Something under the surface follows the pointer, and later a glow takes each world's color** `something-under-the-surface-follows-the-pointer`
  Rung fit: Rung 3, not rung 2: the page has a presence that lives at a low level and notices you. Rung 2 holds still until touched.
  Travels to: An empty state behind frosted glass, a hero that waits for the first action, a dark editor where the pointer carries a faint light.
  Reduce motion: Not changed by the site. For a build: keep a static hand and glow at rest, and let the pointer move them without lag.

### Objects you hold and turn

- **An object idles on its own, takes a push from the pointer, and glides back to its idle** `an-object-idles-on-its-own-takes-a-push-from-the-pointer-and`
  Rung fit: Rung 3. Turning the view by drag stays rung 3, because the viewer steers no object made for this page. Ask first about 3D models.
  Travels to: Loading states, empty states, dashboard headers, map legends, product viewers in shops, a 404 page.
  Reduce motion: One balanced pose (octo-studio f04 1.92s is a good one). Drop the push. Swap drag-to-turn for a few fixed angle buttons, or drop it.

- **One object you grab and turn under a fixed light** `one-object-you-grab-and-turn-under-a-fixed-light`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 when the object sits inside a scene that surrounds you (inknomad). On a normal page it is closer to rung 2 (go-on-white).
  Travels to: Shop product views, part viewers in docs, a live output preview in a tool, a globe on a map, a 3D chart, an avatar or item preview in settings.
  Reduce motion: Show the home pose (inknomad f01 at 0.25 s; go-on-white top-down rest). Keep drag, but follow the pointer one to one with no easing or momentum.

- **Let go, and the thing springs back to its home pose** `let-go-and-the-thing-springs-back-to-its-home-pose`
  Rung fit: Works on every rung. At rung 3 it is the safety rail that lets the page move on its own and still return.
  Travels to: Any draggable view: a map that eases back to north, a rotated 3D chart, a tilted card, a pulled sheet, a zoomed figure in docs, a preview in settings.
  Reduce motion: On release, leave the object where it was, or snap to rest with a short fade instead of a travelling spring.

- **A hero object you spin by hand, ringed by related items, settles to its best face** `a-hero-object-you-spin-by-hand-ringed-by-related-items`
  Rung fit: Rung 3. The object fills the stage and you go in by picking. Free spin leans toward rung 4, but it has no goal.
  Travels to: Shop product pages. Device settings. Profile pages. An article figure with its sources orbiting it. A dashboard focus metric with its drivers orbiting it.
  Reduce motion: Front pose with the cards in a fixed arc. Drag turns it one-to-one with no momentum. A pick swaps with no motion.
