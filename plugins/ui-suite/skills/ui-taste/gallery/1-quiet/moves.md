# Rung 1 motion moves

Generated by a spike script from a motion-reference catalog (the rung 3 motion catalog, both passes). Do not edit by hand; regenerate it.
Copy the feel, never the layout.

17 moves whose rung fit names rung 1.

## Building blocks

### Objects you hold and turn

- **Let go, and the thing springs back to its home pose** `let-go-and-the-thing-springs-back-to-its-home-pose`
  Rung fit: Works on every rung. At rung 3 it is the safety rail that lets the page move on its own and still return.
  Travels to: Any draggable view: a map that eases back to north, a rotated 3D chart, a tilted card, a pulled sheet, a zoomed figure in docs, a preview in settings.
  Reduce motion: On release, leave the object where it was, or snap to rest with a short fade instead of a travelling spring.

### Stages and layouts

- **Build the plain page first, add the immersive layer under one class, and let reduce motion remove only the self-running part** `rung3-carries-a-rung2-page`
  Rung fit: A build rule, not a rung difference. All 10 rung 2 gallery pages already handle prefers-reduced-motion. What is new is the split: motion that follows the hand stays; motion that plays by itself goes. It answers the open question on what a rung 3 page shows under reduce motion. It overlaps the taste rule that every rung keeps a still state.
  Travels to: Every rung 3 page the user builds, as a build rule: write the rung 2 page first, then add the layer under one class.
  Reduce motion: Observed on Apple: content in normal flow, final states at once, films paused on authored stills.

### Physics and weight

- **Depth without outlines: a soft shadow reshapes with the item, and the item's back looks different from its front** `depth-without-outlines-a-soft-shadow-reshapes-with-the-item`
  Rung fit: Usable at every rung. It is what makes rung 3 idle motion read as weight and not a trick.
  Travels to: Drag-and-drop boards, file tiles, map pins being placed, flashcards, shop spec cards with details on the back, settings cards with more on the back.
  Reduce motion: A fixed shadow at the rest height. Flip shows the back face at once.

### Numbers, labels and instruments

- **One toggle pins a small label to every moving item, and labels ride along with them** `one-toggle-pins-a-small-label-to-every-moving-item-and`
  Rung fit: Any rung; it gives rung 3 idle motion something to explain.
  Travels to: Maps, charts, diagrams, floor plans, 3D product views in a shop.
  Reduce motion: Labels appear at once at rest positions.

- **A reading hangs on a thin leader line from the exact spot it describes** `a-reading-hangs-on-a-thin-leader-line-from-the-exact-spot-it`
  Rung fit: Any rung. At rung 3 it rides on a scene that moves on its own and could update with it (not shown here).
  Travels to: Dashboards, maps, data charts, anatomy figures, product specs, docs diagrams.
  Reduce motion: Already still. Keep the line and value; update the number with no count-up.

### Type that moves or holds depth

- **Type and the moving object share one depth stack: the object passes in front, splits the letters or casts a shadow on them** `type-and-the-moving-object-share-one-depth-stack-the-object`
  Rung fit: Works at any rung with a still object. Rung 3 when the object moves on its own through or in front of the type.
  Travels to: Article headers, section openers and dividers on long pages, cover pages, report covers, error pages, dashboard KPI headers, empty states, a shop's product hero, onboarding title cards.
  Reduce motion: A fixed composition with the object in one pose beside or in front of the words, its shadow kept on the type, so the split or overlap never hides a letter. The depth still reads with no motion.

### Rings, tracks and carousels

- **Neighbors peek at both edges so the row goes on** `neighbors-peek-at-both-edges-so-the-row-goes-on`
  Rung fit: Any rung. At rung 3 it frames a row of full scenes.
  Travels to: Shops, period pickers on dashboards, theme pickers in settings, chapter rows in articles, step rows in onboarding.
  Reduce motion: Snap with no glide. The peeking edges still say there is more.

## Feels

### Calm feels

- **Warm, tactile craft: real-feeling objects stacked in depth** `warm-tactile-craft-real-feeling-objects-stacked-in-depth`
  Rung fit: The look fits any rung. Depth plus idle drift lifts it to rung 3.
  Travels to: Article openers, shop collection headers, onboarding, about pages, empty states.
  Reduce motion: Already still.

### Weighty feels

- **Arrive fast, meet friction, rest** `arrive-fast-meet-friction-rest`
  Rung fit: Works on every rung. At rung 3 it carries the camera moves.
  Travels to: Any zoom into a detail: maps, charts, product viewers, docs figures; any element that slides into place.
  Reduce motion: Place the element at rest.

### Snappy feels

- **Leave faster than you came** `leave-faster-than-you-came`
  Rung fit: Every rung; most visible at rung 3 where rooms open and close.
  Travels to: Sheets, modals, detail views, close-ups on maps and charts, any room you step into.
  Reduce motion: A fade on both entry and exit.

## Kinds of page

### Article

- **Article: the opener is a deep scene the reader scrolls down into, and the headline shares its depth** `article-the-opener-is-a-deep-scene-the-reader-scrolls-down`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 in the opener: it surrounds the reader and moves while they choose to scroll. Body text stays at rung 1 or 2.
  Travels to: Feature articles, long reads, case studies, reports, docs home pages, tutorials, changelogs.
  Reduce motion: The opener's final view: all layers at a ratio of 1, the title readable, the figure in its final step.

### Form

- **Form and checkout: submit clears the stage, plays one weighted moment, and comes back as a receipt** `form-and-checkout-submit-clears-the-stage-plays-one-weighted`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 for the one big submit. Field-level feedback stays rung 1 or 2.
  Travels to: Checkout, sign-up, long applications, settings saves that take time, payment confirmations.
  Reduce motion: Cut from the form to the receipt, with a word for the wait.

### Settings

- **Settings: the preview of a setting is alive, and each control changes it at once** `settings-the-preview-of-a-setting-is-alive-and-each-control`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 only if the preview idles on its own. The controls stay rung 1; the pill alone is rung 2.
  Travels to: Theme and density settings, sound and haptics settings, theme pickers, preference panes, simulation tools, chart style editors, design tools.
  Reduce motion: The preview redraws one still frame per change; instant recolor; pill parked; preview at one angle.

- **Settings: options preview themselves on a stage, touring until you choose** `settings-options-preview-themselves-on-a-stage-touring-until`
  Rung fit: Lifts a rung 1 or 2 settings page to rung 3 with a live preview stage.
  Travels to: Settings, theme pickers, plan comparisons, preferences.
  Reduce motion: One option selected; both styles shown side by side.

- **Settings: the subject is the control panel; state shows on the touched part, and the view moves close to it** `subject-is-the-control-panel`
  Rung fit: A tappable drawing with tints is rung 2. Rung 3 adds two things: labels share the picture (difference 6), and the view eases close to the touched part and back (difference 2). Tesla's psi readings with age lines back the first with a still, not a guess. The view easing close stays inferred. Polestar's tile spinner is a rung 1 status slot, not the subject. Overlaps: 'One rich subject on a fixed stage between still panels, with doors into close-ups' and 'A reading hangs on a thin leader line'.
  Travels to: Settings (a device, room or house plan), Form (pick zones on the thing), Dashboard (alerts on the part), Docs (a labeled diagram you can poke).
  Reduce motion: Tints and labels change at once, with no hinge or camera move.

### Map

- **Map and data chart: readings hang on leader lines, live areas twinkle faintly, and the current item draws last** `map-and-data-chart-readings-hang-on-leader-lines-live-areas`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 for the idle twinkle. The leader lines fit any rung.
  Travels to: Route maps, store locators, time-series charts, network graphs, timetables and schedules.
  Reduce motion: Labels and outline in place; points at fixed brightness.

### Empty state

- **Empty state: the object is present but dim and still, or it acts out what is missing** `empty-state-the-object-is-present-but-dim-and-still-or-it`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 only when it moves on its own. The dim still alone is rung 1 or 2.
  Travels to: Empty lists, empty dashboards, first-run tools, search with no results, empty carts.
  Reduce motion: The dim still version; no loop.
