## Building blocks

### Objects you hold and turn

- **Let go, and the thing springs back to its home pose** `let-go-and-the-thing-springs-back-to-its-home-pose`
  Rung fit: Works on every rung. At rung 3 it is the safety rail that lets the page move on its own and still return.
  Travels to: Any draggable view: a map that eases back to north, a rotated 3D chart, a tilted card, a pulled sheet, a zoomed figure in docs, a preview in settings.
  Reduce motion: On release, leave the object where it was, or snap to rest with a short fade instead of a travelling spring.

### Stages and layouts

- **Build the plain page first, add the immersive layer under one class, and let reduce motion remove only the self-running part** `rung3-carries-a-rung2-page`
  Rung fit: A build rule, not a rung difference. All 10 rung 2 gallery pages already handle prefers-reduced-motion. What is new is the split: motion that follows the hand stays; motion that plays by itself goes. It answers the open question on what a rung 3 page shows under reduce motion. It overlaps the taste rule that every rung keeps a still state.
  Travels to: Every rung 3 page the user builds, as a build rule: write the rung 2 page first, then add the layer under one class.
  Reduce motion: Observed on Apple: content in normal flow, final states at once, films paused on authored stills.

### Physics and weight

- **A soft surface hangs from fixed points, sways at idle, and always settles back to one rest pose** `a-soft-surface-hangs-from-fixed-points-sways-at-idle-and`
  Rung fit: Rung 3 when it only sways idle and answers a passing pointer. The demo's grab and drag is rung 4 by the letter of the ladder.
  Travels to: Article cover images, profile banners, shop product previews, a pinned note on a dashboard, the one object in an empty state.
  Reduce motion: Flat on its pins with one baked fold and one corner lifted to show the back. Keep the shadow. No idle ripple, no gust.

- **Light bodies fall, tumble or rain under gravity after one cause, then come to rest in their home spots** `light-bodies-fall-tumble-or-rain-under-gravity-after-one`
  Rung fit: A fall or settle that runs for seconds on its own and fills the view is rung 3. One burst tied to one action is rung 2. Shake as a toy you play with is rung 4. Rung 2 leaning 3: it answers one action, then runs on its own for about 5 s.
  Travels to: Reset filters on a dashboard, restore defaults in settings, re-layout of a board, items landing in a shop tray, an empty state after clear all, footers, success screens, a goal reached on a dashboard, an export done in a tool, a finished signup, an order placed. Dashboards (goal reached), forms (signup finished), tools (export done), learning apps (lesson done).
  Reduce motion: Show the end layout at once through a short crossfade: bodies already at rest by their labels. No tumble, no drop, no confetti; a summary shows at once. No confetti; the summary and a static banner line at once.

- **Depth without outlines: a soft shadow reshapes with the item, and the item's back looks different from its front** `depth-without-outlines-a-soft-shadow-reshapes-with-the-item`

  Travels to: Drag-and-drop boards, file tiles, map pins being placed, flashcards, shop spec cards with details on the back, settings cards with more on the back.
  Reduce motion: A fixed shadow at the rest height. Flip shows the back face at once.
